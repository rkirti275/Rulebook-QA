# Attendance Policy

## 1. Purpose

The University Attendance Policy establishes minimum attendance requirements for students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Regular attendance is considered an essential part of academic learning because classroom participation, laboratory work, tutorials, discussions, and other academic activities contribute to the overall educational experience.

The policy applies to all students unless a specific academic program has an approved additional attendance requirement.

---

## 2. Minimum Attendance Requirement

### 2.1 Standard Attendance Requirement

Every student must maintain a minimum attendance of **75%** in each registered course during an academic semester.

Students whose attendance falls below 75% may be declared ineligible to appear for the end-semester examination in that course.

Attendance is calculated using the total number of scheduled academic sessions conducted by the department.

---

## 3. Attendance Recording

Faculty members are responsible for recording attendance for every scheduled lecture, tutorial, laboratory, practical session, or other compulsory academic activity.

Attendance should normally be recorded at the beginning or during the academic session.

Students are responsible for checking their attendance records regularly through the University's academic portal.

Any discrepancy in attendance records should be reported to the concerned faculty member within seven working days.

---

## 4. Shortage of Attendance

Students who have attendance below the required percentage will receive an attendance shortage notification.

A student with attendance shortage may submit a written request to the Head of Department explaining the reason for the shortage.

The request must include supporting documents where applicable.

The Head of Department may review the request and forward eligible cases to the appropriate academic authority.

---

## 5. Medical and Exceptional Cases

### 5.1 Medical Absence

Students who are absent because of illness may submit valid medical documents to the department.

Medical documents should normally be submitted within seven working days after the student returns to academic activities.

The University may verify medical documents before considering an attendance concession.

### 5.2 Participation in University Activities

Students representing the University in officially approved sports, cultural, technical, academic, or other activities may apply for attendance consideration.

Such participation must be approved by the relevant University authority.

### 5.3 Medical Attendance Exemption

In cases involving serious medical conditions and prolonged medically certified absence, an approved medical exemption may allow a student to appear for an examination with attendance as low as **60%**.

The exemption is applicable only when the required medical documents have been submitted and the case has been formally approved by the competent academic authority.

This provision is intended for exceptional medical circumstances and does not automatically apply to every medical absence.

---

## 6. Attendance Concession Procedure

A student seeking attendance concession must submit an application through the prescribed University procedure.

The application should mention:

- Student name and enrollment number
- Course and semester
- Dates of absence
- Reason for absence
- Supporting documents
- Details of the faculty or department concerned

The department will verify the application before forwarding it to the competent authority.

Approval of an attendance concession is not automatic.

---

## 7. Eligibility for Examination

Before each end-semester examination, the department will prepare an attendance eligibility list.

Students satisfying the minimum attendance requirement will normally be permitted to appear for the examination.

Students below the required attendance percentage may be withheld from the examination unless an approved exemption or concession applies.

---

## 8. Attendance Disputes

Students who believe that their attendance has been incorrectly recorded may submit a written complaint to the concerned faculty member.

If the issue is not resolved, the student may approach the Head of Department.

The decision of the department may be reviewed by the designated academic committee according to University procedures.

---

## 9. Student Responsibilities

Students are expected to:

1. Attend all scheduled academic sessions.
2. Monitor their attendance regularly.
3. Inform faculty members about genuine absences.
4. Submit required documents within the specified period.
5. Avoid providing false or misleading attendance information.
6. Follow department-specific attendance procedures.

---

## 10. Important Rule

The standard University attendance requirement is **75%**. Exceptional medical cases may be considered under the approved medical exemption procedure described in Section 5.3.

All attendance concessions remain subject to verification and approval by the competent authority.
