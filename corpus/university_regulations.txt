UNIVERSITY REGULATIONS
Academic Year 2026

1. GENERAL REGULATIONS

The University establishes academic and administrative regulations to ensure fair, transparent, and consistent procedures for students.

All students must follow the regulations applicable to their academic program, department, examination, hostel, and other University facilities.

Students are responsible for reading official notices and complying with deadlines communicated by the University.

2. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS

Students are normally required to maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course.

Attendance is calculated using the academic sessions scheduled and conducted during the semester.

Students with attendance below the required percentage may be declared ineligible for the end-semester examination unless an approved exemption or concession applies.

3. FEE PAYMENT

Semester tuition fees must normally be paid by 15 August 2026.

Students who fail to pay applicable fees within the prescribed period may be subject to administrative restrictions or late charges.

The University may issue additional notices regarding fee payment deadlines.

4. MEDICAL EXEMPTION

Students facing serious medical circumstances may request an attendance exemption.

The request must be supported by valid medical documentation and must be approved by the competent academic authority.

An approved medical exemption may allow a student with attendance below the normal requirement to appear for an examination.

5. ACADEMIC ASSESSMENT

Students may be evaluated through examinations, assignments, laboratory work, projects, presentations, quizzes, and other approved assessment methods.

The assessment pattern depends on the curriculum of the student's academic program.

Students must complete required academic activities within the deadlines communicated by the department.

6. EXAMINATION CONDUCT

Students must carry their valid University identification and examination authorization where required.

Unauthorized electronic devices, communication, notes, or other materials are prohibited during examinations.

Students must follow instructions given by examination supervisors.

Violation of examination rules may result in disciplinary action.

7. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Students must complete academic work honestly.

Plagiarism, fabrication, impersonation, unauthorized collaboration, and other forms of academic misconduct are prohibited.

Cases of academic misconduct may be investigated by the appropriate University authority.

8. STUDENT APPEALS

Students may submit an appeal when an applicable University procedure provides an appeal mechanism.

An appeal should identify the decision being challenged and provide relevant reasons and supporting information.

Appeals must normally be submitted within the period specified in the relevant notification.

9. HOSTEL REGULATIONS

Hostel residents must follow the entry and exit procedures established by the hostel administration.

The standard weekday hostel gate closing time is 9:00 PM.

Students returning after the prescribed time may be required to provide a valid reason and obtain permission.

Students participating in officially approved University activities may receive special permission for late entry.

10. DISCIPLINE

Students must behave respectfully toward faculty, staff, visitors, and other students.

Damage to University property, threats, violence, harassment, unauthorized access, and serious disruption of University activities may result in disciplinary proceedings.

Students are expected to cooperate with legitimate University investigations.

11. SCHOLARSHIPS

Scholarships may be provided on the basis of merit, financial need, sports achievement, academic achievement, or other approved criteria.

Students must provide accurate information and valid supporting documents.

Scholarship continuation may depend on academic and disciplinary requirements.

12. IMPORTANT ADMINISTRATIVE RULE

Where two University provisions appear to address the same matter differently, the matter should be referred to the competent academic or administrative authority for clarification.

Students should not assume that one provision automatically overrides another without an official interpretation.
