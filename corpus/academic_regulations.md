# SVVV University Academic Regulations 2026

## 1. General Academic Framework

### 1.1 Purpose

The Academic Regulations of SVVV University establish the general rules governing undergraduate and postgraduate academic programs. These regulations describe requirements related to enrollment, attendance, examinations, academic progression, assessment, grading, and completion of academic programs.

Every enrolled student is expected to understand and follow the academic regulations applicable to their program. Students are responsible for checking official notices, academic calendars, departmental instructions, and examination announcements issued by the University.

### 1.2 Applicability

These regulations apply to all regular students enrolled in undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and certificate programs unless a specific program regulation states otherwise.

Where a program has additional requirements, the department may issue supplementary academic instructions. Such instructions must be communicated to students through an official University channel.

### 1.3 Academic Year

The academic year normally consists of two regular semesters. The University may also conduct summer terms, special academic sessions, or supplementary examinations when required.

The dates of commencement, teaching periods, examinations, vacations, and registration activities are published in the academic calendar.

Students are expected to complete registration within the period specified by the University. Failure to complete required registration may result in restrictions on participation in academic activities.

---

## 2. Student Enrollment and Registration

### 2.1 Enrollment

A student becomes an enrolled member of the University after completing the prescribed admission and registration requirements.

Enrollment may require submission of academic documents, identification information, photographs, fee payment, and other documents specified by the University.

The University may verify submitted documents at any stage. If information submitted during admission is found to be incorrect or fraudulent, appropriate disciplinary or administrative action may be taken.

### 2.2 Semester Registration

Students must register for their courses during the registration period announced by the University.

A student who does not complete semester registration within the prescribed period may be prevented from attending classes or appearing in assessments until the registration issue is resolved.

Late registration may be permitted by the University in exceptional circumstances. The University may prescribe a late registration fee.

### 2.3 Course Selection

Students must select courses according to the curriculum applicable to their program and semester.

Students must satisfy prerequisite requirements before registering for a course that has prerequisites.

The academic department may advise students regarding suitable course combinations. However, students remain responsible for checking whether their selected courses satisfy program requirements.

### 2.4 Adding or Dropping Courses

A student may request to add or drop a course during the period specified in the academic calendar.

Requests submitted after the permitted period may require approval from the Head of Department or another authorized academic officer.

Dropping a course does not automatically remove financial obligations associated with the course.

---

## 3. Attendance and Academic Participation

### 3.1 Attendance Recording

Attendance is recorded by the faculty member or another authorized academic representative.

Students are expected to attend scheduled lectures, laboratory sessions, tutorials, practical classes, workshops, and other compulsory academic activities.

Attendance may be recorded using electronic systems, classroom registers, biometric systems, or other methods approved by the University.

### 3.2 Attendance Requirement

Students are expected to maintain the minimum attendance percentage prescribed by the University and their academic program.

For regular academic courses, attendance is calculated using the number of academic sessions attended compared with the total number of sessions conducted.

Students should regularly review their attendance records and report apparent errors to the relevant faculty member or department within the period specified by the University.

### 3.3 Attendance Shortage

A student whose attendance falls below the required level may be declared ineligible for one or more academic assessments.

The department may notify students who have attendance shortages and may provide an opportunity to submit supporting documents where permitted by University rules.

Attendance shortage does not automatically disappear because a student performs well in internal assessments or examinations.

### 3.4 Academic Participation

Attendance alone does not constitute satisfactory academic participation.

Students may also be required to participate in laboratory work, presentations, assignments, tutorials, projects, seminars, and other academic activities.

Failure to complete compulsory academic activities may affect eligibility for assessment.

---

## 4. Assessment and Evaluation

### 4.1 Continuous Assessment

Academic performance may be evaluated through multiple forms of assessment, including quizzes, assignments, laboratory work, presentations, projects, class tests, mid-semester examinations, and end-semester examinations.

The exact distribution of marks is determined by the curriculum and course structure.

### 4.2 Internal Assessment

Internal assessment is conducted during the teaching period.

Faculty members are responsible for communicating assessment requirements to students. Students must submit assignments and complete assessments within the deadlines announced by the relevant course instructor.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks or may not be accepted when the course rules do not permit late submission.

### 4.3 End-Semester Examination

The end-semester examination is conducted according to the University examination schedule.

Students must satisfy eligibility requirements before appearing in the examination.

The examination schedule, examination center, reporting time, and permitted materials are communicated through official examination notices.

### 4.4 Practical and Laboratory Assessment

Courses containing practical or laboratory components may require students to complete experiments, laboratory records, demonstrations, practical examinations, or project work.

A student may be required to complete the practical component separately from the theory examination.

---

## 5. Grading and Academic Progress

### 5.1 Grades

Students receive grades based on their performance in assessments conducted for each course.

The grading system may include letter grades, grade points, marks, or other evaluation methods approved by the University.

The grading scheme applicable to a course should be communicated through the official course information.

### 5.2 Passing a Course

A student must satisfy the minimum passing requirements prescribed for the relevant course.

Some courses may require students to achieve separate minimum requirements in theory and practical components.

Passing one component does not necessarily compensate for failure in another component when separate passing requirements are prescribed.

### 5.3 Academic Progress

Students are expected to make satisfactory academic progress toward completion of their program.

Academic progress may be evaluated using semester performance, cumulative performance, failed courses, credit completion, and other criteria defined by the University.

Students who do not satisfy academic progression requirements may be required to repeat courses, attend additional academic sessions, or follow an academic improvement plan.

---

## 6. Examination Conduct

### 6.1 Examination Hall

Students must report to the examination venue within the reporting time stated in the official examination instructions.

Students must carry the identification or examination documents required by the University.

Students must follow instructions given by examination staff.

### 6.2 Permitted Materials

Only materials specifically permitted in the examination instructions may be brought into the examination hall.

Electronic devices may be restricted or prohibited during examinations.

Students are responsible for checking examination instructions before entering the examination venue.

### 6.3 Academic Integrity

Students must complete examinations, assignments, projects, and other academic work honestly.

Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, impersonation, fabrication of information, use of unauthorized materials, and other forms of academic misconduct may result in disciplinary action.

The University may investigate suspected academic misconduct according to applicable procedures.

---

## 7. Leave and Academic Absence

### 7.1 Academic Leave

Students who are unable to attend academic activities for a permitted reason may submit a leave request through the procedure prescribed by the University.

Approval of leave does not automatically guarantee exemption from attendance requirements or academic assessments.

### 7.2 Supporting Documents

Where supporting documentation is required, students must submit the documents within the prescribed period.

The University may verify submitted documents before approving an absence or leave request.

### 7.3 Extended Absence

A student expecting to remain absent for an extended period should inform the department as soon as reasonably possible.

The department may provide academic guidance regarding missed coursework, assessments, laboratory work, and examinations.

---

## 8. Academic Records

### 8.1 Student Records

The University maintains academic records for enrolled students.

Academic records may include course registrations, attendance records, assessment results, examination results, grades, credits, and academic progression information.

### 8.2 Correction of Records

A student who believes that an academic record contains an error may submit a correction request to the appropriate department or administrative office.

Requests should contain sufficient information to identify the suspected error.

The University may review relevant records before approving any correction.

### 8.3 Confidentiality

Academic records should be handled according to applicable University policies and legal requirements.

Access to academic information may be limited to authorized students, faculty members, administrators, examination officials, and other authorized persons.

---

## 9. Academic Appeals

### 9.1 Right to Appeal

A student may appeal an academic decision where an appeal process is specifically provided by University regulations.

The appeal must be submitted within the prescribed period.

### 9.2 Appeal Procedure

An appeal should clearly state the decision being challenged and the reason for requesting review.

Supporting documents may be attached where relevant.

The authorized academic committee or officer may review the appeal and issue a decision according to University procedures.

### 9.3 Final Decision

Where the applicable regulation identifies an appeal decision as final, no further internal appeal may be available.

Students should consult the relevant departmental or University office for the applicable appeal procedure.

---

## 10. General Provisions

### 10.1 Official Communication

Students must regularly monitor official University communication channels.

Important academic information may be communicated through the University website, student portal, institutional email, notice boards, or official departmental announcements.

### 10.2 Interpretation of Regulations

Where a provision requires interpretation, the matter may be referred to the appropriate academic authority.

Students should not assume that informal advice overrides an official University regulation.

### 10.3 Amendments

The University may amend academic regulations when required.

Amendments should be communicated through appropriate official channels and may become effective from the date specified in the relevant notification.

### 10.4 Responsibility of Students

Every student is responsible for understanding the regulations applicable to their program.

Failure to read an applicable regulation does not normally constitute an exemption from compliance.

### 10.5 Effective Date

These Academic Regulations are designated as the general academic framework for SVVV University for the 2026 academic year.

Where a later officially approved regulation replaces or modifies a provision, the later regulation shall be considered according to the University's prescribed implementation procedure.
