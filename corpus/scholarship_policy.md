# Scholarship Policy

## 1. Introduction

The University Scholarship Policy provides financial assistance to eligible students based on academic performance, financial circumstances, special achievements, or other approved criteria.

Scholarships are subject to eligibility requirements, availability of funds, and approval by the competent University authority.

---

## 2. Types of Scholarships

The University may offer different categories of scholarships, including:

- Merit scholarships
- Need-based scholarships
- Sports scholarships
- Academic achievement scholarships
- Special achievement scholarships
- Government-supported scholarships
- Scholarships sponsored by external organizations

Each scholarship category may have separate eligibility and documentation requirements.

---

## 3. General Eligibility

Students applying for a scholarship must satisfy the eligibility conditions specified for the relevant scholarship.

Eligibility may depend on:

- Academic performance
- Family financial circumstances
- Attendance
- Participation in approved activities
- Sports or cultural achievements
- Previous scholarship status
- Other criteria specified in the scholarship notification

Meeting the minimum eligibility conditions does not guarantee that a scholarship will be awarded.

---

## 4. Merit Scholarship

Merit scholarships may be awarded to students who demonstrate strong academic performance.

The University may consider semester grades, cumulative academic performance, or other approved academic indicators.

The minimum academic performance requirement will be specified in the scholarship notification issued for the relevant academic year.

---

## 5. Need-Based Scholarship

Need-based scholarships are intended to support students who demonstrate financial need.

Applicants may be required to submit documents supporting their financial circumstances.

The University may verify submitted information before approving the scholarship.

False or incomplete information may result in rejection of the application or cancellation of an awarded scholarship.

---

## 6. Sports Scholarship

Students who have represented the University, state, national team, or recognized sporting organization may be considered for sports scholarships.

Applicants may be required to provide certificates or other official evidence of their sporting achievements.

Selection may depend on the level of achievement, performance, and availability of scholarship positions.

---

## 7. Application Process

Students must apply for scholarships using the application process announced by the University.

An application may require:

- Student identification details
- Academic records
- Scholarship category
- Supporting certificates
- Financial documents where applicable
- Bank account information where required

Students should review the application carefully before submission.

---

## 8. Application Deadline

Scholarship applications must normally be submitted before the deadline mentioned in the official scholarship notification.

Late applications may not be considered unless the University announces an extension.

Students are responsible for checking official University communication channels for scholarship deadlines.

---

## 9. Document Verification

The University may verify the information and documents submitted by scholarship applicants.

Applicants may be asked to provide additional documents if clarification is required.

Failure to provide required documents within the specified period may result in rejection of the application.

---

## 10. Scholarship Selection

Scholarship selection is conducted according to the criteria approved for the relevant scholarship category.

Where the number of eligible applicants exceeds the number of available scholarships, the University may apply additional selection criteria.

The final decision rests with the competent scholarship authority.

---

## 11. Continuation of Scholarship

Students receiving a scholarship may be required to maintain specified academic and disciplinary standards.

A scholarship may be reviewed or discontinued if the student fails to satisfy continuation requirements.

Students should check the scholarship notification for category-specific continuation conditions.

---

## 12. Scholarship Cancellation

A scholarship may be cancelled if:

- The student provides false information.
- Required documents are found to be invalid.
- The student becomes ineligible.
- The student violates applicable University rules.
- The student fails to meet continuation requirements.

Any cancellation will be handled according to applicable University procedures.

---

## 13. Multiple Scholarships

A student receiving one scholarship may not automatically be eligible to receive another scholarship simultaneously.

The rules regarding combination of scholarships depend on the specific scholarship schemes and applicable University or government regulations.

Students should disclose existing scholarships when submitting a new application.

---

## 14. Scholarship Payment

Approved scholarship amounts will be processed according to the University's financial procedures.

Payment may be made through the student's registered bank account or adjusted against eligible University fees where applicable.

The timing of payment may vary depending on verification and administrative processing.

---

## 15. Appeal

A student whose scholarship application is rejected may submit an appeal where an appeal mechanism is provided.

The appeal should clearly state the reason for requesting reconsideration and may require supporting documents.

Appeals must be submitted within the period specified by the University.

---

## 16. Student Responsibilities

Scholarship applicants and recipients are expected to:

1. Provide accurate information.
2. Submit documents within the required period.
3. Monitor scholarship notifications.
4. Maintain applicable academic standards.
5. Follow University rules.
6. Inform the University of relevant changes in circumstances.
7. Use scholarship benefits only for permitted purposes.

---

## 17. Final Provision

The University may modify scholarship categories, eligibility criteria, application procedures, or award amounts according to academic and financial requirements.

Students should refer to the latest official scholarship notification for category-specific requirements.
