# Discipline Policy

## 1. Introduction

The University Discipline Policy establishes standards of conduct for students and defines procedures for dealing with violations of University rules.

Students are expected to behave responsibly and respectfully on campus, in classrooms, laboratories, hostels, University events, and other places where they represent the University.

---

## 2. Student Conduct

Students must:

- Respect faculty members, staff, visitors, and other students.
- Follow reasonable instructions issued by authorized University officials.
- Protect University property.
- Maintain academic and personal integrity.
- Follow safety and security requirements.
- Avoid behavior that disrupts academic activities.

---

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

The following activities may be treated as violations of University discipline:

1. Physical violence or threats.
2. Harassment or intimidation.
3. Damage to University property.
4. Theft or unauthorized use of property.
5. Disruption of academic activities.
6. Unauthorized access to restricted areas.
7. Misuse of University information systems.
8. Submission of false information.
9. Violation of examination rules.
10. Repeated violation of hostel regulations.

---

## 4. Academic Misconduct

Academic misconduct includes behavior that compromises the fairness or integrity of academic evaluation.

Examples may include:

- Plagiarism
- Unauthorized collaboration
- Fabrication of academic information
- Using unauthorized materials during examinations
- Impersonation
- Submitting another person's work as one's own

Academic misconduct may be handled under the University's examination and academic integrity procedures.

---

## 5. Reporting a Complaint

Any student, faculty member, staff member, or authorized University representative may report a suspected disciplinary violation.

A complaint should provide sufficient information for the University to understand the alleged incident.

Where appropriate, supporting documents or other relevant evidence may be submitted with the complaint.

---

## 6. Preliminary Review

After receiving a complaint, the designated authority may conduct a preliminary review.

The purpose of the review is to determine whether the complaint requires further investigation.

The University may request additional information from the person making the complaint or from other relevant individuals.

---

## 7. Investigation

If further investigation is required, the competent authority may collect relevant information and communicate with the individuals involved.

The student concerned should normally be informed of the allegation and given an opportunity to provide an explanation.

The investigation should be conducted according to applicable University procedures.

---

## 8. Student Response

A student facing a disciplinary allegation may submit a written explanation within the period specified by the University.

The explanation should address the relevant facts and may include supporting information.

Failure to provide a response within the specified period may result in the matter being considered on the basis of available information, subject to applicable procedures.

---

## 9. Disciplinary Committee

Serious disciplinary matters may be referred to a designated disciplinary committee.

The committee may review:

- The complaint
- Relevant documents
- Statements from involved individuals
- The student's explanation
- Other information considered relevant

The committee may make recommendations to the competent University authority.

---

## 10. Possible Disciplinary Actions

Depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation, disciplinary action may include:

- Written warning
- Formal reprimand
- Requirement to provide an undertaking
- Loss of specific privileges
- Academic or administrative penalties where permitted
- Suspension
- Other action permitted under University regulations

The severity of the action should take into account the circumstances and seriousness of the violation.

---

## 11. Temporary Measures

In circumstances where immediate action is necessary to protect students, staff, property, or the normal functioning of the University, temporary administrative measures may be taken.

Temporary measures are not necessarily a final finding of misconduct.

The matter should continue through the applicable review or disciplinary procedure.

---

## 12. Appeal

A student affected by a disciplinary decision may have the right to appeal where an appeal mechanism is provided.

An appeal should normally:

- Identify the decision being challenged.
- Explain the grounds for appeal.
- Include relevant supporting information.
- Be submitted within the prescribed period.

The designated appellate authority will review the appeal according to University procedures.

---

## 13. Confidentiality

Disciplinary matters should be handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Information concerning an investigation should be shared only with individuals who are authorized or have a legitimate need to receive it.

Confidentiality does not prevent the University from taking necessary action or fulfilling legal and administrative obligations.

---

## 14. False Complaints

Submitting a complaint with intentionally false information may itself be treated as a disciplinary matter.

However, a complaint should not be considered false merely because the allegation could not be established after investigation.

The University should consider the available evidence before taking action regarding a complaint.

---

## 15. Digital Conduct

Students must use University digital systems responsibly.

Unauthorized access, misuse of accounts, disruption of University systems, or deliberate attempts to compromise information security may result in disciplinary action.

Students must protect their account credentials and must not knowingly share access to University systems with unauthorized individuals.

---

## 16. Off-Campus Conduct

Students may be subject to University disciplinary procedures for serious conduct outside campus when the conduct has a direct and significant connection with University activities, safety, or reputation, subject to applicable rules.

---

## 17. Student Responsibilities

Students are expected to:

1. Follow University regulations.
2. Cooperate with legitimate investigations.
3. Provide truthful information.
4. Respect the rights of other students.
5. Protect University property.
6. Follow academic integrity requirements.
7. Use University facilities responsibly.

---

## 18. Final Provision

The University may issue additional disciplinary procedures, notices, or guidelines when necessary.

Students are responsible for following the rules applicable to their academic program, campus facilities, and University activities.
