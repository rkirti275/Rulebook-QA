# Hostel Handbook

## 1. Introduction

The University Hostel provides residential facilities to eligible students. Hostel residents are expected to maintain discipline, cleanliness, safety, and respectful behavior within the hostel premises.

The hostel administration may issue additional instructions when required for the safety and proper management of residents.

---

## 2. Hostel Admission

Students seeking hostel accommodation must complete the hostel application process within the announced period.

Hostel allotment is subject to availability of rooms and eligibility requirements.

Students must provide accurate information during the hostel admission process.

A hostel room may be allotted only after the required administrative formalities have been completed.

---

## 3. Room Allotment

Rooms are allotted by the hostel administration.

Students must occupy only the room assigned to them.

A student must not transfer or exchange a room with another resident without written permission from the hostel administration.

Students are responsible for maintaining the furniture and other facilities provided in their rooms.

---

## 4. Hostel Timings

### 4.1 General Entry Rules

Residents are expected to follow the entry and exit timings prescribed by the hostel administration.

Students leaving the hostel premises should follow the applicable gate-entry and exit procedure.

### 4.2 Weekday Gate Closing Time

On weekdays, the hostel main gate closes at **9:00 PM**.

Residents returning after the gate closing time may be required to provide a valid reason and obtain permission from the hostel staff.

Students should plan their activities accordingly.

---

## 5. Late Entry

A resident who expects to return after the prescribed hostel timing should inform the hostel administration whenever possible.

Late entry may require approval from the designated hostel authority.

Repeated late entry without valid reasons may result in disciplinary action according to hostel regulations.

---

## 6. Weekend and Holiday Rules

Hostel timings may be different on weekends, public holidays, and University holidays.

The hostel administration may publish special timing instructions for such days.

Residents must follow the timing communicated for the relevant day.

---

## 7. Visitors

Visitors are permitted only according to hostel visitor rules.

Visitors may be required to register at the hostel reception or security desk.

Visitors must comply with security instructions.

Residents are responsible for the conduct of their visitors while they are inside the hostel premises.

---

## 8. Special Entry Provision

### 8.1 Academic and University Activities

Students participating in officially approved academic, sports, cultural, technical, or University activities may receive special permission for late entry.

The student may be required to provide evidence of participation in the approved activity.

### 8.2 Weekday Resident Entry

For residents returning from approved University activities, the hostel administration may permit entry until **10:00 PM on weekdays**.

Such permission is subject to verification of the activity and approval by the designated hostel authority.

This provision applies only to approved activities and does not create a general extension of hostel timing.

### 8.3 Emergency Situations

In emergencies, hostel staff may permit entry outside normal timings after verifying the circumstances.

Students should immediately contact the hostel administration or security staff when an emergency affects their expected return time.

---

## 9. Hostel Discipline

Residents must maintain proper behavior within the hostel premises.

The following activities are prohibited:

- Damaging hostel property
- Fighting or threatening other residents
- Creating excessive disturbance
- Unauthorized room changes
- Misusing hostel facilities
- Entering restricted areas
- Violating reasonable safety instructions

Disciplinary action may be taken for violations.

---

## 10. Cleanliness and Hygiene

Students must keep their rooms and common areas clean.

Waste should be disposed of in designated locations.

Students must cooperate with hostel cleaning and maintenance schedules.

Any sanitation or maintenance problem should be reported to hostel staff.

---

## 11. Hostel Property

Furniture, electrical equipment, and other hostel property must be used responsibly.

A student may be required to compensate the University for damage caused through negligence or intentional misuse, subject to applicable University procedures.

Students must not remove hostel property from the premises without authorization.

---

## 12. Safety Rules

Residents must follow hostel safety instructions.

Emergency exits and safety equipment must remain accessible.

Students should report electrical faults, structural problems, fire hazards, or other safety concerns to hostel staff immediately.

Unauthorized modification of electrical connections is prohibited.

---

## 13. Leave from Hostel

Students planning to stay away from the hostel overnight may be required to submit a leave request according to hostel procedures.

The leave request may include:

- Student details
- Dates of absence
- Destination
- Reason for leave
- Contact information where required

The hostel administration may verify leave requests when necessary.

---

## 14. Medical and Emergency Assistance

Students who require urgent medical assistance should contact hostel staff or the designated University support service.

In emergency situations, hostel authorities may take reasonable steps to ensure the student's safety and arrange appropriate assistance.

---

## 15. Complaint Procedure

Students may submit complaints regarding hostel facilities, maintenance, security, or other hostel-related matters.

Complaints should normally be submitted through the designated hostel office or University complaint mechanism.

Students should provide sufficient information to allow the hostel administration to investigate the issue.

---

## 16. Responsibilities of Residents

Every hostel resident is expected to:

1. Follow hostel timings.
2. Carry their University identification when required.
3. Maintain cleanliness.
4. Protect hostel property.
5. Follow security instructions.
6. Respect other residents.
7. Report safety concerns.
8. Follow approved leave procedures.
9. Cooperate with hostel staff.

---

## 17. Final Provision

The hostel administration may issue additional instructions regarding entry timings, visitor management, safety, maintenance, and other operational matters.

Residents must comply with officially communicated instructions applicable to their hostel.
