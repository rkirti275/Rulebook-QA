# Examination Rules

## 1. Purpose

The Examination Rules define the procedures and requirements applicable to internal assessments, practical examinations, end-semester examinations, and other academic evaluations conducted by the University.

All students are expected to follow examination instructions issued by the University, school, department, and examination office.

---

## 2. Types of Assessment

Students may be evaluated through different forms of assessment, including:

- Internal tests
- Assignments
- Quizzes
- Laboratory work
- Practical examinations
- Presentations
- Projects
- Mid-semester examinations
- End-semester examinations

The weightage of each assessment component is determined according to the approved curriculum of the respective program.

---

## 3. Examination Registration

Students must complete examination registration within the announced registration period.

A student may be required to clear applicable academic or administrative requirements before the examination form is accepted.

Students should verify their registered subjects before the final submission of the examination form.

---

## 4. Examination Eligibility

A student must satisfy the academic eligibility requirements prescribed by the University before appearing in an examination.

Attendance eligibility will be verified by the concerned department before the end-semester examination.

Students who do not satisfy the required attendance or approved exemption conditions may be declared ineligible.

---

## 5. Examination Schedule

The examination office will publish the examination timetable through the official University communication channels.

Students are responsible for checking the examination schedule and reporting any timetable-related issue within the announced period.

Changes to the examination schedule may be made by the University when necessary.

---

## 6. Examination Hall Rules

Students must reach the examination venue before the reporting time mentioned in the examination instructions.

Students must carry their valid University identity card and examination authorization or admit card where required.

Students must sit at the seat assigned to them.

Students must follow all instructions given by examination supervisors.

Electronic devices such as mobile phones, smart watches, and unauthorized communication devices are not permitted unless specifically authorized for the examination.

---

## 7. Permitted Materials

Only materials specifically permitted by the examination instructions may be brought into the examination hall.

If calculators, reference material, formula sheets, or other resources are permitted, the examination instructions will specify the conditions under which they may be used.

Students must not use unauthorized notes or communication resources.

---

## 8. Examination Misconduct

Examination misconduct includes:

- Using unauthorized study material
- Communicating with another student during an examination
- Using unauthorized electronic devices
- Impersonating another student
- Obtaining or attempting to obtain unauthorized assistance
- Removing examination material without permission
- Disobeying reasonable instructions of examination staff

Cases of suspected misconduct may be reported to the appropriate disciplinary or examination authority.

---

## 9. Academic Integrity

Students are expected to complete examinations and assessments honestly.

Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, fabrication of information, and other forms of academic dishonesty may result in disciplinary action according to University procedures.

The University may investigate suspected violations before determining the appropriate outcome.

---

## 10. Absence from Examination

A student who is unable to attend an examination because of a serious and unavoidable circumstance should inform the department or examination office as soon as reasonably possible.

Supporting documents may be required.

Approval of an alternative examination opportunity is subject to University rules and the decision of the competent authority.

---

## 11. Practical and Laboratory Examinations

Students must follow the specific safety, attendance, equipment, and submission requirements applicable to practical examinations.

Students may be required to complete laboratory records or practical assignments before being permitted to participate in the practical examination.

---

## 12. Results and Grades

Examination results will be published through the University's designated academic communication system.

Students should verify their results after publication.

Grades are awarded according to the approved grading system of the relevant academic program.

---

## 13. Revaluation and Review

A student who believes that an examination result contains an error may apply for review, revaluation, or verification where such procedures are available.

Applications must be submitted within the period announced by the University.

The applicable fee and procedure, if any, will be communicated with the revaluation notice.

---

## 14. Supplementary and Repeat Examinations

Students who are eligible for supplementary, improvement, or repeat examinations may register according to the schedule announced by the examination office.

Eligibility depends on the academic regulations applicable to the student's program.

---

## 15. Examination Responsibilities

Students are responsible for:

1. Checking the examination timetable.
2. Completing examination registration.
3. Carrying required identification documents.
4. Reaching the examination venue on time.
5. Following examination hall instructions.
6. Using only permitted materials.
7. Maintaining academic integrity.

---

## 16. Final Provision

The examination office may issue additional instructions when required for the proper conduct of examinations.
